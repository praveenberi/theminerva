# Administrator guide
Sign in at `/admin/login`. The sidebar only shows what your role may use.

1. **Settings → Restaurant**: name, contact, address, Google Maps embed, currency, time zone, social links, SEO. **Opening hours** drive the website and reservation slots.
2. **Ordering & tax**: enable dine-in/takeaway/delivery, guest checkout, tax %, service charge, tips, minimum order. **Payments**: tick the methods to offer (online methods need provider keys — see the Integrations tab).
3. **Menu**: menus (Lunch/Dinner), categories (drag to reorder), dishes with photos, sizes, add-ons, allergens, availability windows and recipes (for stock). Changes go live immediately; unpublished or duplicated dishes stay hidden. CSV import/export available.
4. **Tables & QR**: add tables, drag them on the floor plan, download QR codes (PNG/SVG/PDF). Each table QR opens ordering for that table.
5. **Marketing**: coupons (percent/fixed/BOGO, schedule, limits), happy-hour rules, banners, campaigns (opted-in customers only).
6. **Loyalty**: points per currency, redemption value, tiers, referral bonus.
7. **Website content**: hero text, story, policies (draft then publish), gallery with alt text, testimonials, events, review moderation.
8. **Reports**: 17 reports exportable as CSV/Excel/PDF. Dashboard revenue = completed orders; refunds are shown separately.
9. **Staff**: create accounts by role; deactivating signs the person out immediately. The audit log (Settings) records sensitive actions.
10. **Privacy requests**: Customers → open a profile → erase personal data; customers can also export or delete their own data from their account.
