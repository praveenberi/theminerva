# Staff guide
**Kitchen** — open *Kitchen display* (full-screen button for tablets). Tap *Accept*, *Start preparing*, *Mark ready*; tap dishes to tick them off. Red = late. Sound toggle top right.
**Waiter** — *Tables & QR*: tap a table to see the running bill, add an order, split the bill, close the session after payment. The bell shows waiter calls and bill requests.
**Cashier** — open an order, then *Payments*: *Cash received* confirms cash; *Refund* issues full or partial refunds (online refunds go to the provider).
**Delivery** — *Orders* shows only your assigned deliveries; mark *Out for delivery* then *Completed*.
**Manager** — Orders board (drag cards or use buttons), reservations (confirm/seat/no-show), inventory (receive stock, wastage, stock count), staff shifts.
Order flow: New, Accepted, Preparing, Ready, then Out for delivery / Served / collected, then Completed. Online-paid orders can only be accepted after the payment is confirmed.
